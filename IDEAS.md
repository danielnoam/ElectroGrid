# ElectroGrid — gameplay ideas

Ideas to set ElectroGrid apart from other match-3 games, many borrowed from other genres. Nothing here is planned yet;
when one is picked, it moves to TODO.md. Ordered by how much I'd recommend them.

## Top picks

1. **Beat sync (rhythm games).** The grid already pulses and the music is synthwave. Lock the pulse to the music's
   beat, and give swaps made on the beat a bonus (extra combo, or score). Very few match-3 games do this, and it is
   cheap to try: the beat timing and a small window around each beat are most of the work.

2. **Survival as a roguelite (Balatro, Slay the Spire).** At every milestone, pick one of three upgrades that bend
   the rules for the rest of the run, for example:
   - 4 in a row makes a Surge.
   - Line breaks also break Stars.
   - +2 seconds per Plug.
   - Fuses last longer, but blasts are bigger.

   Every run plays differently, which gives Survival real replay value.

3. **Power the circuit (pipe puzzles).** A new objective: the level has a power source and some lamps. Matches lay
   wire on the cells they clear, and you win by connecting power to every lamp. The most "ElectroGrid" idea, a goal
   no other match-3 has.

## More

4. **Boss levels (action games).** A Virus Core with health that corrupts tiles every few moves. Matches and line
   breaks next to it damage it. Fits as the hard "boss" level at the end of each cycle of the level curve.

5. **Blackout levels (horror, stealth).** The board is dark except near powered tiles; matches light up the area
   around them for a few moves.

6. **Gravity flips (puzzle platformers).** Some levels change which way pieces fall, or rotate the board mid-level.

7. **Daily grid (Wordle).** The same seeded puzzle for everyone each day, with a shareable result. Cheap to build and
   good for retention.

8. **Light up a city (meta progression, like Homescapes).** Each finished level powers a district on a neon city map,
   so the level list becomes something you build.

## Suggested order

Prototype beat sync first (small and very noticeable), then power the circuit as a new objective type.
